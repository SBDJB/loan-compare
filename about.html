About Loan Compare

Loan Compare is a free Android app and website from Soluntion Labs that helps people compare personal loans before they apply.

Shopping for a loan usually means visiting lender after lender and trying to line up numbers that are presented differently each time. Loan Compare shows the same facts for every lender: APR range, loan amounts, repayment terms and an estimated monthly payment for the loan you have in mind.

Independence and disclosure

Loan Compare is not a lender or a loan broker. We do not make loans, we do not make credit decisions, and we do not take loan applications. When you follow a link to a lender, you leave Loan Compare and deal directly with that lender.

We are not affiliated with any lender shown on this site. No lender has endorsed Loan Compare, pays to be listed, or pays us when you click a link, apply, or take out a loan. The links to lenders are ordinary links with no tracking or referral code.

If that ever changes, for example if we join a lender's referral program, we will say so clearly on this page and next to the lenders concerned before any paid link goes live.

How lenders are chosen and shown

  • We list a selection of well-known US personal loan lenders. It is not the whole market, and being listed is not a recommendation.
  • APR ranges, loan amounts, terms and fees are taken from each lender's own public website, and the comparison page shows the date they were last checked. Lenders change their terms, so always confirm the details on the lender's site.
  • Lenders that fit your loan are ordered by their lowest published APR.
  • Monthly payment figures are estimates calculated from the lender's APR range for the amount and term you enter. They are not offers.
  • We do not list loans with an APR of 36% or higher, or loans that require full repayment in 60 days or less.
  • Lender names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify them.

Not financial advice

The guides and tools here are general information to help you compare. They are not advice about your personal situation. Consider speaking to a qualified adviser or a non-profit credit counselor if you are unsure whether to borrow.
