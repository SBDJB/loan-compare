      How to read this

      • The APR range covers every borrower a lender approves. The lowest rate goes to the strongest applicants. Your own rate depends on your credit, income and debts. See how your credit score affects loan rates.
      • Monthly payments are estimates worked out from each lender's lowest and highest APR. They are not offers, and they do not account for an origination fee taken out of the loan. See personal loan fees explained.
      • This is not the whole market. It is a selection of well-known US lenders whose top APR is below 36%. Being listed is not a recommendation.
      • Check your actual rate by prequalifying on the lender's site, which normally uses a soft credit check. See prequalification versus applying.
